Welcome to the AI Auditing Task
About the AI system
The AI system predicts whether a person is likely to be charged with another crime within the next two years.
For each person, it gives one of two predictions:
  • High Risk – More likely to be charged with another crime.
  • Low Risk – Less likely to be charged with another crime.
What information does the AI use?
  • The data comes from people who were arrested in Florida (USA). It includes information about each person, such as their age, sex, and criminal history.
  • You will explore a sample of these records during the task.
  • The data also includes information about what happened within the following two years. You will not see this information directly, but the tool uses it to calculate measures of how well the AI's predictions performed.
